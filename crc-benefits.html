KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Support with
the details in view.

What kind of group is described in the referral guide?+

The 2025 CRC referral guide specifies five or more enrolled medical employees. Confirm the group’s current fit and market availability with the CRC team.

Are these brochures intended for business-owner distribution?+

The two CRC-branded flyers are marked for licensed insurance agency use only. Use them to prepare agent conversations; request appropriate employer-facing material when needed.

Does every group receive the same service package?+

Services and arrangements depend on the group and current agreement. The roles and tools summarized here describe the supplied Account Services model rather than a guaranteed staffing allocation.

What is the best Dreams next step?+

Discuss the opportunity with Mike so the handoff follows the appropriate CRC path. The newer referral guide includes CRC’s referral contact and the information the team requests.
