EVERYDAY CARE

Help is closer than they think.

The supplied guides describe virtual urgent care and services that can help employees navigate common, non-emergency needs.

  • Virtual urgent care and behavioral health services
  • Prescription benefits, subject to the current formulary
  • Virtual veterinary support described in the program materials

PROTECTION

An added layer of support.

Limited indemnity benefits can provide defined payments for covered events, subject to the applicable plan.

  • Hospital indemnity, emergency room, and ambulance benefits
  • Accident and critical illness benefits with specific restrictions
  • Inpatient surgery and anesthesia benefits described in the summary

WELLNESS

Small steps. More everyday support.

The program materials bring wellness education and personal development into the benefits conversation.

  • Health-risk assessments and wellness education
  • Stress, sleep, and smoking-cessation resources
  • Zig Ziglar coaching described in the overview

FAMILY SUPPORT

Look beyond the employee alone.

The supplied materials describe family access to telemedicine and prescription benefits. Review who is covered and how each service works.

  • Confirm covered family members in the selected plan
  • Review service access, exclusions, and benefit limits
  • Use the employee support guide to explain the next steps

Quantum supplements existing coverage; it does not replace major medical insurance. Benefits, eligibility, exclusions, covered family members, and limits depend on the applicable plan documents.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

A clearer path
to the next conversation.

Does Quantum replace major medical insurance?+

No. The supplied materials describe supplemental fixed-indemnity benefits and related services. Review the employer’s major medical coverage separately.

Is there one employee minimum or guaranteed savings amount?+

The supplied documents use different employee minimums and illustrations. Confirm current eligibility through a workforce and payroll review, and use an employer-specific proposal instead of a blanket savings promise.

Are the accident and critical illness benefits unrestricted?+

No. The detailed handout includes benefit-year limits, age restrictions, waiting periods, and exclusions. Review that handout and the full current plan documents before describing coverage.

Where should payroll information go?+

Use the provider’s approved secure process described in the proposal guide. This site has no payroll upload form; arrange the next step with Mike or the program team.
