RESEARCH ASSOCIATES

Participation is part of the picture.

Research Associates contribute health and consumer insights through activities described in the program. Eligibility for benefits depends on active status and current requirements.

  • Surveys, videos, quizzes, health coaching, and other activities
  • Review employment, participation, and good-standing requirements
  • Timely contributions and selected plan terms matter for coverage

RECRUITERS

A clear, approved recruiting path.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

The details deserve
a closer look.

Is LifeX simply an individual insurance policy?+

The supplied FAQs describe employment as a Research Associate with access to group medical and wellness benefits for eligible active associates. Review the employment relationship and plan separately; LifeX is not described here as the insurance carrier.

Are research activities or good standing relevant?+

Yes. The guides link benefit access to the Research Associate relationship and good standing. Review current activity and participation requirements; do not assume that no activity is required.

Can I use the HSA rate sheet as a current quote?+

No. Although the supplied filename says 2026, the cover states an effective date of January 1, 2025. Obtain current rates, availability, exclusions, and full plan documents before recommending or enrolling.

Does the Connected Care appointment link also appoint me for LifeX?+

No. These are separate program paths. Speak with Mike about the approved LifeX recruiting entity, licensing requirements, and current onboarding process.
