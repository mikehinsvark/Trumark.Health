A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Clarity before
your next step.

Ask Mike a question ↗
Which link gets me appointed with Connected Care?+

Use the dedicated Connected Care contracting portal and code Dreams1 when requested. The separate life and annuity form below serves a different appointment path.

Does applying mean I am appointed?+

Submitting your information starts the process. Follow the portal’s confirmation and review steps, and confirm your approved status before conducting activities that require the appointment.

How should I use the compensation calculators?+

Select the title or level you already qualify for, then enter hypothetical payable enrollments. An employee count alone does not establish a title. The results are gross illustrations using the referenced rates and assumptions; the current written compensation agreement controls.

What does “life of the contract” mean?+

Renewal compensation continues only as provided by the applicable agreement, while the account remains active and payable and you continue to meet its requirements. It does not mean guaranteed lifetime income or guaranteed client retention.

Where do life and annuity appointments go?+

The Dreams Insurance Solutions, Vantage, and TruSolutions life and annuity appointment path uses this separate appointment form. The Connected Care code and contracting link above are for Connected Care.
